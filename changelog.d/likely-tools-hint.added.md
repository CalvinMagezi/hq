**HQ is nudged toward the right tool before it guesses.** When a message clearly needs a tool that is not loaded yet, such as making a spreadsheet, setting a reminder or sending an email, HQ now gets one short line naming the likely tools, so it loads the right one instead of doing the job by hand or searching blindly. The match runs on the device with no model call and looks only at your message and the list of tools, never at what you have used before.
