**Tiles and plots can read another note, and plots draw three more ways.** A `tile` or `plot` with `from="[[Stock]]!D2:D40"` now reads that note's sheet from the same vault (by file name, or `[[Folder/Stock]]` when two notes share a name), with `where`, `group` and `format` working as before. A `plot` also takes `type=area`, `stacked` or `scatter`, and the `.xlsx` export writes them as native charts.
