**Comments work offline in the web app.** With no connection, the Comments sheet still lists the comments as last saved on the device, and you can add or resolve a comment. It shows at once, marked "Waiting to send", and goes to HQ in order when the connection is back, without ever adding the same comment twice. If the note changed in the meantime and HQ refuses one, your words stay in the sheet with HQ's sentence, a Copy text button and a Discard button. On a shared computer nothing waits and Add and Resolve stay off while HQ cannot be reached.
