**Word comments survive a shrink.** When the suite shrinks a Word file, the file's review comments now come across as `comment` blocks right after the paragraph or table they were on, with the author, the date and whether the thread was resolved. Comments that sit in a header, a footer or a note cannot be placed and are counted as a loss in the report. A plain `hq vault import` is unchanged. The loss report also counts tracked changes now: inserted text is kept, deleted text is dropped.
