**Reminders that ring at the time you chose.** HQ can now hold a reminder for a clock time in your own time zone, ring it as an inbox item that is pushed, and keep reminding you until you answer. A reminder can repeat (daily, weekdays, chosen weekdays, monthly), and a "nudge" follows up after 30 minutes, 2 hours and on the next two mornings before it gives up, waiting out your quiet hours. Done and Snooze answer a ping from the inbox. Set one with `hq reminders add`; check that they are ringing with `hq reminders doctor`. No model is involved, so a reminder costs nothing to ring. Chat tools and the phone screen follow.
