**Set a reminder by asking in any chat.** "Remind me at 4 to call Moses, and keep reminding me until it's done" now works from the phone, the web, Telegram and Discord, and from any MCP client. HQ answers with the time it set in your own words, and knows your time zone from the first phone that reports one. Setting a reminder needs no approval and costs no model call. The Reminders screen's routes (`/api/reminders`) and a per-turn "what time it is for you" line for the model come with it. `watch_create` now points to reminders for "at 4pm" requests.
