**Price research gives the unit people use, two sources and a data-quality note.** A new built-in skill, `local-price-research`, guides requests that compare prices across places: it finds how the good is sold locally and gives that unit beside a per-standard-unit figure, looks for two independent sources per place, records source, date and retail or wholesale on every figure, briefs a verifier helper with the source URLs, and marks a number unverified when no one could open its source. Sheets built from web data now carry those columns too.
