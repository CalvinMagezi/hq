**What HQ remembers can now be searched by the words of a message.** HQ keeps a short summary of your chats. It now has a search index over those summaries, the people and places they name, and their topics, plus the details needed to tell where a memory came from. This change builds the index only; nothing in chat uses it yet. Older memories are indexed on the first start after the update. Memories from before the update that came through Discord are marked as unattributed, because Discord can carry other people's messages, and they will not be brought up on their own until you review them. Memories saved with a numeric timestamp by an older version are rewritten as text so the daily forgetting pass can read them.
