**The chat always says what HQ is doing, and offers next steps.** While a reply is being written, the line above it names the tool being prepared (a long note can take minutes to write), says when helpers have reported back, and shows the steps done so far, instead of a bare "HQ is working". After a research or build reply, two to four next steps (add more places, email the sheet, make a PDF) appear above the box. They are chosen on your device from what the reply did, with no model and nothing stored.
