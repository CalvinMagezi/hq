**Settings has a new page, "What HQ remembers".** It lists the short notes HQ has saved from your chats, newest first, with how old each is and anything unusual about it (pinned, held back, out of date, from a shared channel, edited by you). You can search them and filter to the ones that need a look. Open a note's options to edit it, pin it so it never ages out, mark it out of date, keep one HQ held back because it came from a shared channel, or forget it. Forgetting asks first, deletes the note and everything HQ made from it, and tells you plainly what it could not reach: the chat it came from and older backups. The page also says how notes reach your chats right now. It is in the Safety and limits group, it is not shown in HQ Lite, and only you can open it.
