**HQ checks your notes for links that lead nowhere.** Every few hours HQ reads your notes and finds links to notes that are not there, links that could mean more than one note, links to headings that are gone, notes with the same text and properties it cannot read. When something needs a look, Notes shows a short card, and Review lists each item with the note and line so you can open it or ignore it. Nothing is changed for you. `hq vault health` prints the same list, `hq doctor` shows one line about it, and chats can ask for it ("Check my notes for broken links"). It reads notes on your device only: no AI model, no network. Turn it off with `daemon.vault_health: false`.
