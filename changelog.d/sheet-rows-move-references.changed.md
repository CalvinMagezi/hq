**Inserting or deleting a sheet row now works on sheets with formulas.** Adding a row in the middle or deleting one used to be refused whenever a formula, tile or chart read the rows below. HQ now moves those references the way Excel does: totals cover a row added inside their range, ranges shrink when rows go, and a reference to a deleted row shows `#REF!`. Tiles and charts follow too. The only refusal left is deleting every row a tile or chart reads.
