**Reminders on your phone.** A reminder that rings is a card in Needs you, first after a sign-in, with Done and two snoozes (in an hour, tomorrow morning). The same Done and Snooze are buttons on its notification, and they work without turning on notification details or unlocking the phone. The notification shows what you asked to be reminded of, even on the lock screen, unless you turn that off in Settings, Reminders. That page lists what is coming (overdue, today, tomorrow, later) and what has finished, and sets a new reminder with a quick time or a picked day and time, a repeat, and "keep reminding me until I mark it done".
