**A Docs section on the public site.** `agent-hq.online/docs/` lists the project's own guides by who you are (starting out, looking after your HQ, on a server, for developers), with a search box and a page for each guide. The pages are built from the markdown in the repository, so they cannot drift from it, and the build stops on a doc without a summary or audience or a link to a file that does not exist.
