**A Reminders page on the web, and Done and Snooze on a reminder in the Inbox.** Settings, Work, Reminders lists what is coming (overdue, today, tomorrow, later) and what has finished, and sets a new reminder: a time, whether it repeats, and whether HQ should keep reminding you until you mark it done. Each reminder has Done, Snooze (in an hour, this evening, tomorrow morning) and Stop. When a reminder rings in the Inbox, its detail has the same Done and Snooze buttons. Times are worked out in your own time zone, not the browser's.
