**A private ledger from your mobile-money messages.** `hq finance import <export.json>` turns MTN and Airtel transaction messages you export into reviewable drafts in a Sheets note, and `hq finance check` tells you how well the amounts agree with the balances the messages report. The ledger is a normal Suite sheet with totals and a spending chart, kept in a folder agents cannot read, and nothing in it is sent to a model, a search index or another device. Nothing reads your inbox: you choose the file.
