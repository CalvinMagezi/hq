**You can now see, correct and delete what HQ has learned from your chats.** HQ saves short summaries of your chats, but until now there was no way to look at them. In the terminal: `hq memory list` shows them with their age and anything unusual about them (pinned, held back, out of date, from a shared channel), `hq memory search <words>` finds one, `hq memory view <number>` shows everything about it, and `hq memory edit <number> "<text>"` corrects it, which makes it yours. `pin` stops one from ever aging out, `outdated` keeps it but stops it being brought up, and `keep` vouches for one HQ held back because it came from a shared channel. `hq memory forget <number> --yes` deletes it and everything HQ made from it (the insight notes written from it, their lines in `MEMORY.md`, its name on concept pages, and its entry in the archive of old memories). HQ keeps only a fingerprint of the words, so it does not learn the same sentence again, and tells you what it could not reach: the chat it came from and any earlier backups. `hq memory export` prints everything as JSON.
