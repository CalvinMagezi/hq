**HQ writes down what matters, once.** Say "remember that ..." and HQ keeps that sentence for good, even if it is about health or family. Without it, HQ no longer saves turns about health, belief, politics, sexuality or passwords on its own. Saying the same thing twice reinforces one note instead of adding a copy. If the model that writes notes is down, HQ still saves the first sentence you wrote. Text that tries to give HQ new orders is held back for you to look at.
