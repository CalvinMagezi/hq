**Fix links from the Inbox, the web app and your phone.** When HQ is sure about some broken links, one Inbox question offers to fix them, and approving it does. After a first yes it asks once whether to keep doing the certain fixes without asking. The Notes screen on the web and on the phone shows **Fix** and **Undo** on its card, the web Review page puts a repair beside each item (marked **Sure** or **Likely**), says which notes were left alone and why, and lists recent fixes with Undo, and the phone has the same list on a Note check page. All of it stays on your devices: the weekly note and the question are never sent through a push service.
