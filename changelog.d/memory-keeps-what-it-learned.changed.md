**HQ no longer deletes what it learned from your chats on its own.** Until now HQ lowered the importance of every saved note each day and deleted the weak ones for good about two months after they were saved, so most of what it learned quietly disappeared. Now nothing is deleted unless you forget it. A note that goes a year without being brought up is put away (never one you pinned), and HQ keeps at most 20,000 in use. A note put away is still listed and searchable, under "Over a year old" on the What HQ remembers page or with `hq memory list --archived`, and you can bring it back with "Use it again". This change also stops two kinds of notes from being brought up on their own: ones held back for you to look at, and ones from a shared channel HQ could not tie to you. HQ's insights are still written as notes, but it no longer adds them to your `MEMORY.md`, which stays the file you write. Old stored copies of whole chat turns, which nothing read, are cleared.
