**Sheets on the phone got a real toolbar.** Tap a cell to pick it and type in the formula bar (Enter or leaving the field keeps it). Press and hold a cell, or tap the picked one, for a sheet of actions: edit, copy and paste a range, fill down, column type, insert and delete rows and columns (shown only when this HQ supports them), and a card view of one row. Sort and filter change only what you see, and the first column can stay in view. Cells that the browser cannot work out show the value HQ worked out.
