**Slides you can arrange.** A real 16:9 stage and a strip of small pictures of each card. Press and hold a card to drag it to a new place, or use Move earlier and Move later. Add, copy and delete a card (with Undo), pick a layout, a transition and colours with a warning when the text is hard to read (under 3 to 1), and write speaker notes. Present mode takes a swipe or a tap on the left or right side, asks for full screen, and keeps the arrow keys.
