# When HQ stops in the background

HQ on an Android phone runs as an app that Android is allowed to pause to save battery or memory. If it stops, nothing you wrote is lost. HQ simply is not answering until it starts again.

## What you will see

HQ tells you when Android may have stopped it: "Android may have stopped it to save battery, or the phone ran low on memory." Open the app and it starts again.

## The settings that matter

Open **Keep running** in the app. It checks what it can and lists what to change:

- **Battery use.** Set it to **Unrestricted**. If it says "Optimised, so Android may pause it", that is the one to change.
- **Remove permissions if app is unused.** Turn it off. Otherwise Android can take HQ's permissions away if you do not open it for a while.
- **Notifications.** Keep them on, so a question from a helper reaches you.

On a Samsung phone there is one more setting in Samsung's own battery menu. The app cannot check that one, so it tells you how many settings are fine and that one needs you to look.

The menus carry different names on different phone brands. Search your phone's settings for "battery" and then "app battery" or "background usage" to find the same switches.

## A charger helps most

A sleeping phone that is unplugged is the hardest case, and it is not yet shown to answer in time every time. If you want HQ to reply while you sleep, leave the phone on a charger, or run HQ on a server and use the phone as the remote. See [Deploying HQ on Hetzner](HETZNER.md).
