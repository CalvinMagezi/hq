# What HQ asks you, and why

HQ can read and write your Vault and your tasks without asking. Anything that reaches outside them waits for you. That is the whole rule, and this page explains what it looks like.

## Where the question appears

In your **Inbox**, as a card that says exactly what HQ wants to do. On the phone you also get a notification, and in the web app a push if you turned them on. Nothing happens until you answer.

## What waits for you

In plain words, HQ asks before it:

- runs a command or changes a file on a computer;
- starts a coding helper, or sends something to another machine or agent;
- changes HQ's own settings, or installs an update;
- commits to git or opens a pull request for you;
- uses your other accounts, such as Google, or a tool from a connected server;
- exports a note to a place on disk;
- changes or deletes an event on your phone's calendar. This one always asks, even in a clean chat, and the card shows the event and the change.

The card shows the exact tool and the exact details, and your answer covers that one request only. Approving one thing never approves the next.

## How to answer

- **Allow once** lets that one request run.
- Declining stops it, and HQ says that nothing ran.
- Leaving it alone is safe. An unanswered request is pushed a second time after 15 minutes. If it expires with no answer, the chat that asked is told that nothing ran.

## Why HQ asks

Text from the web, an email or a document can try to talk HQ into doing something you did not ask for. Asking about each outside action keeps you the one who decides. After a chat has read outside text, HQ is stricter still: it narrows what a helper it starts may do.

## Quieting the questions you do not need

You can silence a topic, such as helpers or tips, or pause them all. Silenced items still arrive in the Inbox, with no push, no notification and no badge. A question about something HQ itself wants to do can never be silenced, so a new kind of approval is never hidden by accident.

For the exact list of calls and the rules behind them, see [Owner approval](security/OWNER_APPROVAL.md).
